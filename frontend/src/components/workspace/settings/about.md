# Matrix-Law Legal Workspace

Matrix-Law Legal Workspace is a professional workspace for lawyers and law-firm teams.

It helps with legal research, matter material organization, document drafting, contract review, compliance analysis, file management, and authorized capability usage inside the legal workspace.

---

## Product Positioning

- Legal-work first: every capability is presented around lawyer workflows and law-firm operations.
- Evidence-aware: legal conclusions should distinguish facts, inference, and recommendations.
- Governed execution: models, tools, and skills are used only through authorized capability policies.
- Workspace-oriented: case files, documents, tasks, and assistant conversations stay aligned with matter work.

## Support

For product support, contact your law-firm administrator or the platform operator configured for this deployment.
