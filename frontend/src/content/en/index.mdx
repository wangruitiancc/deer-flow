# Matrix-Law Documentation

Matrix-Law is a web-based legal workspace for law firms and lawyers. It supports
case work as well as focused legal matters such as consultations, contract
review, legal research, and document drafting.

## Product boundaries

- Every runnable conversation is bound by the server to an authorized firm,
  legal matter, owner, and matter membership.
- Models, MCP services, Skills, and upstream credentials are managed by the
  platform. Firm members can narrow the access they receive.
- Legal materials, OCR text, citations, and human review stay in the law firm's
  data domain.
- AI output is a draft for review by an authorized legal professional; it is
  not a final legal opinion.
