# Why Matrix-Law

Legal work depends on the matter, the people authorized to handle it, and the
materials that support each conclusion. Matrix-Law brings those parts into one
web workspace for lawyers and law firms.

## Work in a matter-specific space

Each conversation is connected to a legal matter on the server. Tenant,
membership, and matter permissions are checked before a run starts, so a page
selection or browser-supplied identifier cannot grant access by itself.

The workspace supports ongoing cases and focused work such as a consultation,
contract review, legal research, or a draft. Lawyers can organize source
materials, inspect cited passages, and review generated work in the context of
the matter.

## Keep authority and data boundaries clear

The platform manages model, MCP, and Skill access. A firm can narrow the
permissions granted to its members, while members cannot attach private model
keys or replace platform-managed endpoints.

Legal materials, OCR text, citations, and human review remain in the firm's data
domain. Where an external service is not configured or authorized, the system
fails closed instead of silently routing materials elsewhere.

## Keep a lawyer in control

Matrix-Law helps organize research, evidence, and drafting work. Generated
content remains a draft for review by an authorized legal professional. It is
not represented as a final legal opinion, and a successful technical run does
not by itself establish legal accuracy.

The current local two-server setup is a candidate environment. Production
deployment, real-provider operations, and real-material quality require their
own acceptance evidence.
