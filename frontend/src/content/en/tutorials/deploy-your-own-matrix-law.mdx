# Run the Matrix-Law Local Candidate

The authoritative Matrix-Law deployment sources are in `deploy/two-server/`.
This page describes two local-only containers that simulate the capability and
law-firm tenant servers for development and acceptance. The cloud target is native
systemd services on two independent Linux hosts; Docker/Compose is not the
production deployment architecture.

## Start the local candidate

Use the [Quick Start](/docs/application/quick-start) page for the single maintained
command sequence and local URLs. `start.sh` accepts only
`TWO_SERVER_DEPLOYMENT_MODE=local-candidate`; it is not a cloud deployment command.

## What this candidate verifies

The two containers have separate databases, Redis instances, service credentials,
and data boundaries. Candidate checks can verify local authentication, routing,
authorization, synthetic workflows, and container health. They do not establish
production PKI or secret-store readiness, real-provider quality, legal-material
quality, customer billing, or disaster recovery.

Do not use real legal materials or enable external providers until their data
processing, retention, residency, human-review, and operational requirements have
been accepted. Production deployment, signing, backup, restore, and launch readiness
require their own evidence and procedures.
